Privacy Policy

Candy Pop Universe for Android (Google Play)

Developer: aetherion labs Country: Nepal Last Updated: October 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Candy Pop Universe (the "Application" or "App"), created and published by aetherion labs ("we", "us", or "our"), based in Nepal. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy.

This policy explains what information is collected when you download, install, and play Candy Pop Universe on Android through the Google Play Store, how that information is utilized, and your choices regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. Candy Pop Universe does not require you to create an account with your real name, home address, or telephone number. The information handled by the app falls into the following categories:

A. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and preferred system language.
  • Gameplay & App Analytics: Game level progression, completed puzzle milestones, high scores, coins/boosters utilized, session length, and frequency of gameplay.
  • Technical & Crash Diagnostics: Crash logs, stack traces, and system performance metrics to help us fix glitches and maintain stability.

B. AI Feature Interactions

Candy Pop Universe includes AI-powered gameplay features (such as smart level puzzle assistance, level recommendations, and creative candy themes) utilizing the Google Gemini API.

  • When you engage with an AI feature, your input text or puzzle state request is transmitted securely to Google's Gemini API server to generate the real-time response.
  • We do not collect or request personally identifiable information through AI prompts. Please do not enter any sensitive personal data (such as full names, passwords, or personal credentials) into any AI feature text inputs.
What we do NOT collect: We do NOT collect your GPS location, contact list, camera or microphone data, SMS, financial bank account numbers, or government-issued IDs.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Candy Pop Universe integrates trusted third-party SDKs to provide advertising, game distribution, and AI intelligence. These third parties may collect and process information in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

Provider Purpose Information Processed Privacy Link
Google AdMob In-app advertising (banner, interstitial, rewarded video ads) Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, general country/city region, ad interaction events Google Ads Policy
Google Gemini API AI gameplay generation, puzzle hints, and level design features In-game user prompts, contextual puzzle board state Google Privacy Policy
Google Play Services App distribution, crash logs, billing, and system API support Hardware info, OS version, app installation instance IDs Google Play Privacy

Advertising Choices: Google AdMob may serve personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your consent and device settings. You can manage, reset, or delete your Advertising ID at any time via your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

4. How We Use Collected Information

The information processed is used solely to:

  • Provide, run, and maintain the game mechanics and features of Candy Pop Universe;
  • Process AI puzzle hints and dynamic candy challenges via Google Gemini API;
  • Serve relevant in-app advertisements via Google AdMob to support ongoing development;
  • Identify bugs, monitor crashes, and optimize Android device performance;
  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions and prevent fraudulent use.

5. Data Security

We employ standard industry measures to safeguard your information. Communication between Candy Pop Universe, the Google Gemini API, and Google AdMob occurs over encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols. However, please note that no method of electronic transmission over the internet or mobile network is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Candy Pop Universe is NOT directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 for individuals within the European Economic Area / UK, or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).

We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately at supremacharya67@gmail.com. We will take prompt steps to investigate and remove any such data from our records.

7. User Rights & Data Control

Depending on your place of residence, you have certain rights regarding personal data:

  • Nepal: Users in Nepal are protected under the Individual Privacy Act, 2075 (2018). You have the right to request access to and correction of any personal data stored about you.
  • EEA / UK (GDPR): You have the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of your personal data, and the right to object to processing.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): California residents have the right to know what personal data is collected, request deletion, and opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To exercise any of these rights, or if you have questions regarding data collected by third-party partners like AdMob, please contact us at supremacharya67@gmail.com.
